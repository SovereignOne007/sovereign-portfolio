03 / Feature development

THE PAWN

Original fantasy feature screenplay

The smallest piece can change the entire game.

THE PAWN explores transformation, historical truth, and the consequences of individual choice. Its central metaphor is drawn from the chessboard: a pawn may appear limited, but its journey can alter the board — and it can become something far greater than others expect.

The story is shaped as an ensemble, with characters whose decisions carry independent weight. The creative focus is not simply on who holds power, but on who chooses to act, what truth costs, and how conviction can transform into consequence.

About the creator

Howard J. Mills

I am a writer, published author, and independent intellectual-property creator focused on developing original stories for screen and expanded media. My work combines creative writing training with hands-on development of scripts, story worlds, character arcs, and presentation materials.

I studied Creative Writing at Full Sail University and have independently published fiction. My professional background also spans technical support, customer experience, operations, and coordination — experience that informs a practical, organized approach to creative work.

My goal is to build original properties with a strong narrative identity, memorable characters, and the depth to sustain an audience beyond a single story.
